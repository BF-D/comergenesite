ComerGene Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Who we are

ComerGene is a personalised-nutrition app. It reads a nutrigenomics report you already own and turns it into wellness guidance - meal plans and trait scores, not medical advice.

There is no separate legal entity named on this page. Use the developer contact on the App Store or Play listing until a public contact address exists.

What the app does with a DNA report

You choose a file (BGI Genomics, 23andMe, AncestryDNA, a raw genotype export, a VCF, or a zip of one of those). ComerGene reads that file so it can map nutrition traits.

  • The file is parsed in memory.
  • Only the 72-locus nutrition panel is kept for scoring.
  • The upload buffer is discarded after parsing.
  • ComerGene never sequences DNA and never stores a genotype.
  • What may persist is the derived trait result - a one-way projection. You cannot reconstruct a genome from it.

What we store

  • Derived nutrition trait scores and the meal plan built from them.
  • On-device preferences you enter (for example age, sex, height, weight, diet notes) so nutrient targets can be calculated.
  • We do not store the original report.
  • We do not create an advertising profile.
  • We do not sell data.

Health data (those derived trait scores) is used only for app functionality: showing your traits and building a meal plan. It is not used for tracking and is not linked to an account - ComerGene does not require you to sign in.

What we do not do

  • No third-party advertising networks.
  • No tracking pixels or advertising identifiers.
  • No sale of personal or health data.
  • No sharing of genotype data (it is not kept).
  • No claim that results are diagnostic or clinical.

On-device storage

The iOS app uses UserDefaults (via AsyncStorage) to remember onboarding and derived results on this device. When you pick a report, the system document picker gives the app access to that file; file timestamps may be read for a file you selected.

Deletion

You can delete an analysis at any time in the app (Profile → Privacy & data). That request is permanent. The API call is DELETE /v1/analyses/{id} and is idempotent: repeating it does not leave leftovers.

If you cannot use the in-app control, contact the developer through the App Store or Play listing and ask for deletion.

Children

ComerGene is rated 12+. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not upload someone else's report unless you have the right to do so.

Wellness, not medicine

Everything ComerGene shows is wellness and lifestyle guidance. It is not a diagnosis, a disease-risk score, or a clinical recommendation, and it does not replace a qualified clinician.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above and this hosted page.

Contact
